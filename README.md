# Visual Progression: Van's Organ Jars

A RimWorld 1.7 mod. Organs your colony harvests before it reaches the industrial age are Van's organ jars. Organs
harvested after are the game's organ box. Organs nobody harvested, such as loot and quest rewards, are the game's box.
Organs that traders sell, and that other factions' pawns carry, follow that faction's age. A retexture or upscale of the
game's organ box shows wherever the box does. With Expanded Prosthetics and Organ Engineering - Forked, its stomach,
eyes, noses, ears and jaws change the same way. Its arms and legs can't be harvested, so they look like any organ you
didn't harvest.

[Steam Workshop page](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3838085030)

Something has to raise your colony's tech level as you play, such as Vanilla Factions Expanded - Tribals. Without one,
your colony stays at the age it starts in, so its organs never change.

## Requirements

[Visual Progression Framework](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3834891951) 1.4 or later and
[Van's Retexture : Organ jars](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3541295970).
[Expanded Prosthetics and Organ Engineering - Forked](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1949064302)
is optional.
